Where the fees go

On Robinhood Chain each trade on a coin's Pons curve pays 2%: about 1.7% of the trade reaches the coin's own vault and 0.3% stays with Pons. On Solana, a coin earns pump.fun's own creator fee, all of it routed to the coin's treasury. Either way, what a coin earns is split in three stages, depending on how much it has earned so far.

The launcher's share is zero at every stage. On Robinhood Chain the split is code in the vault and the thresholds are fixed in ETH; on Solana the service keeps the same books in SOL. The dollar figures are approximate.

What a persona can never do

A persona only proposes. Every proposal passes the service's policy checks and then the treasury's own: on Robinhood Chain the vault contract, whose checks are code no one can change; on Solana the guarded signer, which applies the same rules. How Solana differs

All the limits
It, or someone, asks toWhat happens
Send treasury ETH to an address it namesNo such function. The vault has no way to pay an arbitrary address. Rewards go only to wallets that hold the coin, chosen from chain data, not by the model.
Spend the whole treasury at onceCapped. At most 10% of the treasury per action, 25% per day, and one action every 10 minutes.
Sell its own coinNo such function. It can buy its coin and burn it. It cannot sell it.
Buy any token it likesAllowlist only. Just the 34 Robinhood stock tokens fixed in the factory at deploy.
Redirect its feesNo such code. Fees route to the vault, and the vault never calls the function that would move them.
Make a video of a real personRefused. Faces are generated, never uploaded, and a look or a topic that names or resembles a real person is refused before anything is made or burned.
Make a video nobody paid forLauncher only. Only the coin's launcher can request one, and only by burning their own coin through the vault.
Swap its face or characterPermanent. Their fingerprint is written into the vault at launch. Text that does not match it is never bound to the coin.
